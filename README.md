## Salvador Catsis

Computational scientist working on mathematical models of real systems — NHS
treatment pathways, epidemics, physical processes. I enjoy developing
mathematical models of real-world systems and then building software to simulate
them. I'm particularly interested in how far a model's predictions hold once the
real system stops behaving ideally.

Currently looking for part-time or contract work in modelling, simulation and
data analysis.

**Python · Julia · MATLAB · CUDA · OpenCL**
